About

Where telecom operations meet modern software

At Nepal Telecom I work in the IT Department's Software & Security Wing. I build the internal and customer-facing systems that support a national operator: complaint and fault ticketing, document tracking, procurement, and portals for staff and retirees.

Outside NTC I build commerce products. That includes GtradeA, a cross-border import platform for Nepal, and an AI-powered fashion storefront with vector search.

I also teach. My project-based courses take engineers from PyTorch fundamentals to production AI agents.
